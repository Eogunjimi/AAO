Usable capacity, measured honestly

A lead-acid bank advertised at 200 Ah gives you far less than 200 Ah in practice, because discharging it deeply destroys it. LFP lithium lets you use the large majority of rated capacity every single day without shortening its life, which is why a smaller lithium bank routinely outperforms a larger lead-acid one.

We size on the kilowatt-hours you actually need between sunset and sunrise, install with proper ventilation and battery management, and tell you the usable figure rather than the flattering one on the label.

Lithium Battery Storage
questions, answered.

Typically ten years or more in normal use. LFP cells are rated for upwards of six thousand cycles, and at roughly one cycle per day that is well over a decade before capacity drops to the point of replacement. Lead-acid banks in the same duty commonly need replacing within two years.

Enough to carry your overnight load with margin. We total the appliances that must run between sunset and sunrise, multiply by the hours they run, and add headroom for cloudy days. That number, not the size of your roof, determines the bank.

LFP is the safest widely available lithium chemistry and is markedly more thermally stable than the cells used in phones and laptops. Installed in a ventilated location with an integrated battery management system, it is appropriate for indoor installation.
