Power that stays on when the grid does not

Most Nigerian homes do not need a bigger system — they need the right one. We start with an appliance-by-appliance assessment: what you run, when you run it, and what genuinely has to stay on during an outage. That load profile drives every decision that follows.

Tier-1 panels feed a hybrid inverter and a lithium battery bank sized to carry your essential circuits overnight. Changeover is seamless, so the fridge, the lights and the Wi-Fi never notice the grid has gone.

Cost follows system size, and size follows your load. A small essentials-only system covering lights, fans, TV and Wi-Fi sits well below a whole-home system running air conditioning and a borehole pump. We quote only after the appliance assessment, so the number you receive is the number you pay.
