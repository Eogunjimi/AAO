ABOUT.

We started re-roofing homes and ended up powering them. Same crew, same obsession with doing it right — now measured in gigawatt-hours.

EST. 2014 — LAGOS 3 GENERATIONS 62 CREW & ENGINEERS

THREE GENERATIONS OF BUILDERS. ONE OBSESSION: DO IT PROPERLY.

( ORIGIN )

2014. A father, son and daughter of builders, re-roofing houses across Lagos. Clients kept asking the same question: "while you're up there — can it also power the house?" We spent two years learning solar the way we learned roofing: properly, or not at all.

( CREW )

62 people today — engineers, drone surveyors, electricians and installers. Salaried and certified, never day-labour with a ladder. The crew on your roof has done this hundreds of times, and their name is on the warranty.

( PROMISE )

Fixed quotes, honest yield models, Tier-1 materials and a 10-year workmanship warranty we honour without small print. If a number we promised ever misses, we make it right. That's the whole brand.

( THE YARD )

BUILT LIKE A FAMILY BUSINESS. BECAUSE IT IS ONE.

Every Helios system is surveyed by drone, modelled to the kWh, and signed off by a named engineer. We grow by referral — most new roofs come from an old customer waving a neighbour over the fence.
