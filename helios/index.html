It's simpler than it sounds! Solar panels installed on your roof absorb sunlight and convert it into clean electricity. Your inverter then changes that electricity into the standard power your home or business uses for appliances. Any extra power produced during the day is stored safely in high-capacity lithium batteries so you have seamless, uninterrupted power through the night or during grid outages.

Yes, absolutely! Solar can easily run high-energy appliances like air conditioners, refrigerators, water pumps, televisions, and lighting. Because we design before we install, we carefully assess your property's specific load requirements first. This ensures your system is sized correctly to run everything you need reliably and safely.

Yes, they do! Solar panels generate power from ambient daylight, not heat. While power generation may be slightly lower on heavy rainy days, your system will still absorb sunlight. Plus, our reliable lithium battery storage stores energy during sunny periods so your lights, fans, and essentials stay powered no matter the weather.

Your backup time depends entirely on your system size, battery capacity, and the appliances you run. During our free consultation, we ask about your desired backup hours and custom-size a lithium battery storage system that comfortably covers your night-time or power-outage needs.

Once we've completed your custom energy assessment and finalized your design, the physical installation typically takes 1 to 2 days. Our team works neatly and efficiently to ensure zero disruption to your home or business routine.
