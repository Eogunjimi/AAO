# 14. FAQ (dark)
- **Title (H2):** FAQs from Nigerian solar customers.
- **Q&A pairs:**
  1. **How does a solar energy system actually work?** — It's simpler than it sounds! Solar panels installed on your roof absorb sunlight and convert it into clean electricity. Your inverter then changes that electricity into the standard power your home or business uses for appliances. Any extra power produced during the day is stored safely in high-capacity lithium batteries so you have seamless, uninterrupted power through the night or during grid outages.
  2. **Can solar power my entire home or business?** — Yes, absolutely! Solar can easily run high-energy appliances like air conditioners, refrigerators, water pumps, televisions, and lighting. Because we design before we install, we carefully assess your property's specific load requirements first. This ensures your system is sized correctly to run everything you need reliably and safely.
  3. **Do solar panels still work on rainy or cloudy days in Lagos?** — Yes, they do! Solar panels generate power from ambient daylight, not heat. While power generation may be slightly lower on heavy rainy days, your system will still absorb sunlight. Plus, our reliable lithium battery storage stores energy during sunny periods so your lights, fans, and essentials stay powered no matter the weather.
  4. **How long will my battery backup last at night or during an outage?** — Your backup time depends entirely on your system size, battery capacity, and the appliances you run. During our free consultation, we ask about your desired backup hours and custom-size a lithium battery storage system that comfortably covers your night-time or power-outage needs.
  5. **How long does the installation process take?** — Once we've completed your custom energy assessment and finalized your design, the physical installation typically takes 1 to 2 days. Our team works neatly and efficiently to ensure zero disruption to your home or business routine.
  6. **Can I start with a smaller backup system and upgrade later?** — Yes, definitely! We build modular, future-proof systems. If you're working with a specific budget, you can start with a basic Inverter & Lithium Battery setup to replace your generator during grid outages, and we can seamlessly add solar panels or additional batteries as your energy needs expand.
  7. **How much money can I really save on generator fuel?** — Most of our residential and business clients see a 70% to 90% drop in fuel and generator maintenance costs within the first month. Instead of spending money every day on fuel that disappears, your investment goes into a long-lasting asset that pays for itself over time.
  8. **What kind of warranty and after-sales support do you offer?** — We believe in building long-term relationships, not just one-time sales. All our solar panels, smart inverters, and lithium batteries come with valid manufacturer warranties. More importantly, we provide 100% professional installation and dedicated after-sales support whenever you need help, maintenance, or advice.
