The inverter is the part of your system that decides whether the lights flicker. We specify it against surge behaviour and real load, install it with proper protection, and configure it so changeover is something you never notice.

(03) SERVICE INVERTER RATING 1.5 – 30 kVA CHANGEOVER <20 ms
THE HEART OF THE SYSTEM

Rated for the surge, not just the average

Undersized inverters are the most common fault we are called out to fix. A fridge compressor, a borehole pump or an air conditioner draws several times its running current at start-up, and an inverter chosen on running watts alone will trip or fail early.

We specify on surge behaviour as well as continuous load, install with correct breakers, surge protection and earthing, and configure the changeover so critical circuits never see the gap. Pure sine wave output throughout, because sensitive electronics deserve it.
