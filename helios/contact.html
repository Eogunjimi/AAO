Any extra power produced during the day is stored safely in high-capacity lithium batteries so you have seamless, uninterrupted power through the night or during grid outages.

Yes, absolutely! Solar can easily run high-energy appliances like air conditioners, refrigerators, water pumps, televisions, and lighting. Because we design before we install, we carefully assess your property's specific load requirements first. This ensures your system is sized correctly to run everything you need reliably and safely.
