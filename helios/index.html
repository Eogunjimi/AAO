Nigerian solar customers.

Most residential systems are two days on site: day one rails, panels and cabling; day two storage, inverter and commissioning. Scaffolding is gone before the weekend. C&I projects run to a phased programme agreed up front.

Hybrid systems switch to stored power in under 20 milliseconds — your TV won't blink. At night the battery carries the load; when it's empty and the grid is down, the generator input (if fitted) takes over automatically.

Yes — 0% finance up to 24 months on residential systems, and CAPEX or PPA structures for commercial projects. Most clients spread the system across the months it's already saving them money.

Dust costs yield, so we schedule a rinse-and-inspect plan through harmattan months — included free for the first two years on every system. Rain does the rest of the year's cleaning for free.

Our 10-year workmanship warranty covers everything we did: mounting, cabling, commissioning. Panels carry their own 25-year manufacturer performance warranties, which we register and manage on your behalf.
