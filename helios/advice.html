( SEP 2026 — STORAGE )

Sizing a battery for Lagos load-shedding

The most expensive mistake we fix is a battery sized from a brochure, not from a load audit. Storage should be sized against your actual night-time load — the hours between sunset and the morning's first kettle — not against the size of your panels.

DO THIS FIRST

  • List what actually runs at night: fridge, freezer, TV, fans, routers, pumps, AC (be honest).
  • Multiply watts by hours to get your night kWh. A typical Lekki home lands between 6–10 kWh.
  • Add 20% headroom for battery round-trip efficiency and old-fridge surprises.
  • Keep the AC on its own circuit — if you must run it at night, size for it separately or not at all.

Rule of thumb from the yard: 5 kWh of LFP per bedroom that sleeps with the AC off, and a serious conversation if it sleeps with the AC on. Our surveys measure this with a week of logging before we quote a single cell.

( AUG 2026 — DESIGN )

Why all-black panels are winning Nigerian roofs

Ten years ago, all-black modules cost you 4–6% efficiency for the look. Today's N-type all-black cells have closed that gap to under 2%, and on a visible roof the aesthetic dividend is real: an array that reads as architecture adds value at resale in a way a blue-grid patchwork never did.

WHAT WE SPEC, AND WHY

  • All-black cells with black backsheets and dark frames — no silver gridlines shouting from the street.
  • Concealed cabling and black MC4 runs; the difference is in the details people feel but can't name.
  • Rail kits torqued and marked — a pretty array that leaks is just a pretty leak.

The one place we still spec standard blue-cell modules: ground-mount arrays behind a hedge, where nobody sees them and every naira should go to yield. Looks where it's seen, yield where it isn't.

( JUL 2026 — C&I )

Diesel vs solar: the C&I math in 2026

Every facilities manager knows the fuel bill; fewer know the true runtime cost. Add maintenance, oil, filters, depreciation and the night security of a generator that dies at 2 a.m., and diesel's effective cost per kWh in Lagos sits far above the pump price suggests.

THE SPREADSHEET, SIMPLIFIED

  • Diesel effective cost: fuel + service + downtime risk, per kWh actually delivered.
  • Solar levelised cost: capex ÷ (25 years × annual yield), typically a fraction of diesel.
  • Hybrid strategy: solar carries the base load, the generator becomes backup for the 3% of hours that need it.

On our 2026 C&I projects, paybacks have landed between 2.6 and 3.4 years. After that, the roof is simply profit. Bring us your fuel logbook — we'll show you the number in one meeting.
