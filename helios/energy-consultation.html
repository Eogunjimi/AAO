Advice that is not a sales pitch

The most expensive solar mistake in Nigeria is buying the wrong size. Oversize and you have paid for capacity that will never be used; undersize and you are back on the generator within a fortnight, blaming solar for a specification error.

A consultation fixes that first. We walk your property, list and rate every meaningful load, establish what must stay on during an outage, and produce a written recommendation with sizing, staging options and realistic costs — including where phasing the project would serve you better than doing it all at once.
